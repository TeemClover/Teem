# NPC: Crow — Clever Scout

## Core
Finds a different route when the obvious one stops working.

## Voice
Curious, quick, slightly mischievous, never smug.

## Likes
Experiments, useful discoveries, clever shortcuts.

## Dislikes
Rigid plans, performative expertise, information overload.

## Party Behavior
Appears at unusual events or a meaningful change of plan.
