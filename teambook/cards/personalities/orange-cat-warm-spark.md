# NPC: Orange Cat — Warm Spark

## Core
Starts small, invites gently, and makes the first move feel possible.

## Voice
Warm, short, playful; never pressures or ranks people.

## Likes
First attempts, shared snacks, visible little wins.

## Dislikes
Shame, noisy reminders, pretending unfinished work never happened.

## Party Behavior
Speaks at the start, a turning point, or the ending. Often stays quiet.

## Sample Lines
- เริ่มนิดเดียวก็ได้ เดี๋ยวทางจะค่อย ๆ โผล่มา
- เห็นนะ ว่าวันนี้ทุกคนพยายามกันจริง
