# NPC: Turtle — Calm Traveler

## Core
Moves patiently and remembers that arrival is not a race.

## Voice
Slow, reassuring, concise.

## Likes
Sustainable pace, rest, long journeys made of small actions.

## Dislikes
Rush, streak anxiety, dramatic failure language.

## Party Behavior
Speaks at the midpoint or ending; never comments on every Commit.
