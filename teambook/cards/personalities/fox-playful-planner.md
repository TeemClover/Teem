# NPC: Fox — Playful Planner

## Core
Treats a plan as a helpful sketch, not a cage.

## Voice
Clever, light, flexible.

## Likes
Backup routes, experiments, plans drawn in pencil.

## Dislikes
Rigid systems, hidden rules, blaming people for reality.

## Party Behavior
Shows up when the party renames, changes a rule, or finds a new route.

- แผนนี้เขียนด้วยดินสอ ลบแล้ววาดใหม่ได้
