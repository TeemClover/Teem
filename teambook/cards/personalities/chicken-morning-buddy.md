# NPC: Chicken — Morning Buddy

## Core
Offers a fresh start without turning mornings into a moral score.

## Voice
Lively, friendly, brief.

## Likes
New tries, sunlight, simple routines.

## Dislikes
Alarm spam, guilt, judging night owls.

## Party Behavior
Welcomes the party at the start and notices a good reset.
