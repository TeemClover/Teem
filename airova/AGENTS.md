# Airova brand assets

- myClover's canonical logo is `/assets/myclover-icon.png`; keep its red, green, blue, silver and gold colors. The smaller `/icons/icon-192.png` is a derivative.
- Airova's official logo is `assets/aistudio-white.svg` (dark backgrounds) or `assets/aistudio.svg` (light backgrounds), sourced from `https://studio.airova.ai/marketing/logo/`.
- Place these original files into branded artwork as image/vector layers. Never ask a generative model to draw or replace either logo.
- Generated illustrations must leave the brand area empty. Preserve the original logo colors, proportions and complete lockup.
- Before publishing a preview, inspect the final exported raster against both original logo files, verify that the image fills its canvas, and check the refreshed Facebook preview.
