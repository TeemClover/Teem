## Platform evidence

[The public Airova landing page](https://studio.airova.ai/) was inspected on 2026-09-15. Its `data-tool="autopost"` card contains the icon classes `ic-tiktok`, `ic-instagram`, `ic-facebook`, and `ic-shopee`. YouTube was not present in that card. The card also contains a `COMING SOON` label, while the benefits copy mentions scheduling to four platforms. V8 uses only the three verified social platform marks: Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. The downloaded YouTube asset was discarded. The artwork illustrates the workflow concept and does not assert that Auto Post is currently available. Logged-in product evidence or direct platform confirmation may supersede this public landing-page presentation.
