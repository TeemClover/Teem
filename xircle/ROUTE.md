# Route guard — /xircle/

Updated 2026-11-09. Current owner direction: a company product experience that X-VISORs can open to explain the system to customers. This supersedes the personal appointment-first V3 journey. The user no longer works as an X-VISOR; the website must not sell their personal services.

## Journey

**Scroll overview → Sleep → Eat/Food AI → Move → Body → App/Band/Scale → RoutineX → White Cat knowledge → official Xircle system.**

- Root: `/xircle/`; `/Xircle` is an alias, not another implementation.
- Active runtime: `company-story.js`, `company-story.css`; optional decorative `orb-field.js` (WebGL2). The V3 personal sample engine is retired.
- A sticky illustration follows the chapter being read. Native scrolling and fragment links remain in control; no wheel interception or forced scroll progression.
- Presentation mode exposes the same product content as 7 focused topics. Native dialog semantics, arrows/Home/End/Escape, deep links, Back/Forward and refresh are supported. It gathers no customer information.
- App/Band/Scale have keyboard-ready tabs; RoutineX and FAQ use native details. Content remains readable without JavaScript or WebGL and with reduced motion.
- Primary company destination: `https://buy.xircle.health/login?ref=O5F3LN`. Do not replace login with register or invent prices/conditions.
- White Cat is the knowledge room at `/xircle/learn/`, not an external notebook or care-room funnel.
- No personal Meet CTA, Teem/Ako presentation, TeamBook handoff or optional game invitation belongs in the XIRCLE product path. Other products elsewhere in the repository are outside this change.

## Compatibility and state

Old entry paths use `legacy-entry.js` + `route-contract.js` to reach this product experience. Bounded explicit Compass focus can point to a product topic. Retired invitation/notebook parameters are ignored. Do not read, migrate, clear or rewrite legacy browser records. No example choices are fabricated or sent as customer health data.

`#appointment` now resolves to `#start`; `#experience` and `#fallback` resolve to `#sleep`. These anchors explain products and do not imply an appointment, purchase or completed assessment.

## References and claims

The 49 reference pages open directly. Shared static markup is generated by `build/journey-shell.mjs`; `journey.css` / `journey.js` own shared navigation and reading layout. `reference.js` loads artwork only. `knowledge.js` maintains search/filter state in the URL, without a new browser-storage key.

Reuse existing images as illustrations. Keep product/source boundaries, avoid diagnosis or guaranteed outcomes, and check official current labels/conditions before adding claims or prices. The top-level `/maxage` sleep-kit page remains outside this change.

Run the commands in [JOURNEY_QA.md](JOURNEY_QA.md) before delivery. Do not infer production deployment from a local test or branch preview.
