DESIGNED BY SUBTRACTION

We removed game design.
Then added back emptiness.

Numbers, resources, random draws, text walls and power growth all delay the first meaningful choice. First Hand removes them. Red, Green and Blue resolve conflict. Silver begins without a fixed power—and that deliberate openness creates timing, sacrifice, bluffing and doubt.

HUMAN-DESIGNED · AI-ASSISTED PRODUCTION

Designed on paper.
Proven with people.

Every rule began with a game designer, physical paper and real tables. The system was refined through hundreds of plays with real people—from young children to senior players—until the rules could disappear and the choices could remain.

The idea existed long before today’s AI tools. During the project’s early stage, AI helped one designer prototype visual directions, write the online game system and communicate the idea quickly and beautifully. It did not replace game design, physical prototyping or human playtesting—it finally gave one person enough hands to carry a long-held idea into the world.

FAQ

Questions we would ask before backing.

Can the game really begin in 7 seconds?

Yes—the claim is 7 seconds to begin, not 7 seconds to memorize everything. The visual Rule Card remains visible. Point to the color loop, choose 1 of 7 cards and reveal together.

Do both players need a box?

No. One box contains enough Word Cards and one Rule Card for every player at a 4-player table.

Are there exactly 3 official games?

Yes. HERO’S DUEL, CORE7 and 三川 MIKAWA are the complete official line for First Hand. Game 4 and beyond are open for player-crafted rules.

Is official play really 0% RNG?

Yes. No dice, no draw luck and no random resolution. Uncertainty comes from hidden human decisions.

Why buy the cards if the rules are open?

Because the physical deck is the art, language and portable ritual. The mechanic is open; the artifact earns the purchase.

How is shipping charged?

We ship worldwide. Shipping and applicable taxes are collected separately after the campaign through the Pledge Manager.
