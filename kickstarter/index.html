THE DESIGN METHOD

We did not build the game by adding rules.
We found it by removing them.

The system begins with four colors and seven cards chosen by each player. Red, Green and Blue form a closed victory cycle. Gray interrupts it. Nothing is drawn at random and no card is stronger because it costs more.

BODY gives the game energy.
SOUL gives it meaning.
MIND gives it direction.
CRAFT gives players the power to make what comes next.

That is why the product is not only a deck. It is a small language that can carry games, conversation and new rules from one table to another.

FAQ

Questions we would ask before backing.

Do both players need a box?

No. One box contains enough Word Cards and one Rule Card for each player at a 4-player table.

Are there exactly 3 official games?

Yes. HERO’S DUEL, CORE7 and 三川 MIKAWA are the complete official line for First Hand. Game 4 and beyond are open for player-crafted rules.

Is the game really 0% RNG?

Yes in official play: no dice, no draw luck and no random resolution. Uncertainty comes from human decisions.

Why buy the cards if the rules are open?

Because the physical deck is the art, language and portable ritual. The mechanic is open; the artifact earns the purchase.

Does rarity make a card stronger?

Never. Foil and special art are cosmetic only.

How is shipping charged?

We ship worldwide. Shipping and applicable taxes are collected separately after the campaign through the Pledge Manager.
