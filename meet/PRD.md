# myClover Session — Product Requirements Document

Status: Draft for approval  
Product surface: `myclover.com/meet/`  
Document owner: myClover  
Updated: 30 August 2026

## 1. Product decision

`/meet` will become the digital entrance to a **myClover Session**: a short, valuable conversation with Teem and Ako that begins a relationship before asking the visitor to buy, work with us, or understand the wider myClover universe.

The landing page remains editorial, calm, and lightweight. The booking experience becomes conversational: visitors answer one short question at a time through choices that feel like the beginning of a real conversation.

The product must communicate one promise:

> Bring one thing you are thinking about. Meet two people who see it from different sides. Leave with one clearer next step.

Thai expression:

> **หนึ่งเรื่องของคุณ · สองมุมมอง · หนึ่งก้าวถัดไปที่ทำได้จริง**

## 2. Why this can only be a myClover Session

The differentiation is not a generic health consultation, a network-business invitation, or a conventional sales presentation. It is the combination of two complementary perspectives.

### Teem

- 19 years building health-care systems around people and teams.
- Has trained thousands of people through real-world, primarily offline work.
- Sees structure, roles, strengths, and the system required to continue.

### Ako

- Builds advanced routines that can be adapted to different daily lives.
- Understands products, taste, food, and what people can realistically sustain.
- Makes clean food that is still genuinely enjoyable.

### Together

> Teem sees what can help a person go further. Ako sees what that person can live with every day.

The session is valuable because the visitor does not have to choose between a strategic system and real life.

## 3. Problem

myClover already has multiple useful products, pages, communities, tools, and paths. New visitors currently face three barriers:

1. They do not know which destination is relevant to them.
2. Too much information before trust feels like a sales presentation.
3. A normal booking form collects details but does not begin a relationship.

The new experience must make the visitor feel, before submitting:

- “They are starting from me, not from a pitch.”
- “Meeting these two people will be worth my time.”
- “I can be honest and still decide not to continue.”

## 4. Target visitors

### A. Health and routine

People who want to understand their body, eating, movement, sleep, energy, or sustainable routine.

### B. Licensed-care path

People who are independently interested in sitting the relevant licence examination and want to understand how to prepare, what the role requires, and which systems support continuing customer care. Teem and Ako do not recruit or enrol the visitor. If the visitor chooses this path for themselves, myClover can provide exam preparation and a routine-tracking app system. Passing the examination is never guaranteed.

### C. Open conversation

People who are curious about Teem and Ako, want a fresh perspective, or do not yet know how to describe what they need.

## 5. Goals

### Primary goal

Increase qualified meeting requests with Teem and Ako.

### Supporting goals

- Make the value of the meeting clear within the first screen.
- Let visitors self-select the conversation that is relevant to them.
- Reduce fear of being pressured into a purchase or business decision.
- Collect enough context for Teem and Ako to prepare.
- Make the act of booking feel like the first minute of the session.

### Non-goals

- Explaining the complete myClover universe.
- Publishing the full X-Visor deck.
- Diagnosing health conditions.
- Promising health outcomes or income.
- Replacing Teem and Ako with an AI adviser.
- Pretending that a scripted interaction is a live human conversation.

## 6. Product principles

1. **People before products.** Tools appear only after the visitor's intent is known.
2. **One question at a time.** The visitor should never face a long form.
3. **Specific value, honest boundaries.** State both what the visitor receives and what is not promised.
4. **Fast enough to respect attention.** Motion creates rhythm but never delays progress.
5. **A request is not a confirmed appointment.** Use accurate status language until a time is confirmed.
6. **Elegant, not empty.** Restraint must still provide enough proof to justify the visitor's time.

## 8. Page architecture

The page must remain short enough to scan in under two minutes.

1. **Hero** — promise, proof, real photograph, primary CTA.
2. **Intent selector** — Health, Licence & Care System, Open Conversation.
3. **Dynamic value panel** — concrete takeaways and an honest qualifier for the selected intent.
4. **The Duo** — Teem and Ako as two perspectives converging on the visitor.
5. **Session method** — Listen, See, Connect, Choose.
6. **Licensed-care path closed folder teaser** — shown only after the visitor selects the examination and care-system path.
7. **Honest questions** — answer objections before booking.
8. **Conversational booking** — one question at a time.
9. **Signature** — “ใครเจอเรา คนนั้นโชคดี” with its meaning explained.

The page must not add a directory, global navigation, product grid, or full ecosystem map.

## 10. Conversational booking

### Interaction model

- Opens from any booking CTA without navigating away.
- Appears as a focused conversation layer above the landing page.
- Identifies itself as `myClover Session`, not as a live Teem or Ako chat.
- Uses short, scripted pacing to make each response feel acknowledged.
- Offers tap choices wherever possible; free text is used only for name, contact, and optional context.
- Shows a discreet progress indicator and always allows Back/Edit.

### Required flow

#### Step 1 — Intent

Message:

> ก่อนลงนัด ขอรู้จักคุณนิดหนึ่ง วันนี้อะไรพาคุณมาหาเรา?

Choices:

- `อยากดูแลสุขภาพ`
- `สนใจสอบใบอนุญาตและระบบดูแล`
- `ยังไม่แน่ใจ แค่อยากรู้จักกัน`

After selection, the system gives one short, intent-specific acknowledgement explaining how Teem and Ako will approach that topic.

#### Step 2 — Meeting mode

Message:

> อยากเริ่มเจอกันแบบไหน?

Choices:

- `ออนไลน์ · 25 นาที`
- `เจอจริง · 45 นาที`
- `Coffee / Buffet` for relevant intents
- `เจอกัน + Body Check-in` for Health

#### Step 3 — Requested time

Message:

> ช่วงไหนที่คุณอยากเก็บไว้ให้เรา?

P0 behavior:

- Show real Gregorian dates for the next 14 days.
- Show clear time windows or configured request slots.
- Label the choice as a requested time until Teem or Ako confirms it.

P1 behavior:

- Connect to true availability and reserve a confirmed slot.

#### Step 4 — Identity and contact

Messages:

> อยากให้เราเรียกคุณว่าอะไร?  
> แล้วให้เรายืนยันนัดทางไหนดี?

Required fields:

- Preferred name.
- LINE ID or telephone number.
- Optional context, maximum 200 characters.

#### Step 5 — Review and submit

Display:

- Selected topic.
- Meeting mode and duration.
- Requested date and time.
- Contact channel.
- Edit controls for each answer.

Consent:

> ฉันเข้าใจว่านี่เป็นการพูดคุยและข้อมูลเบื้องต้น ไม่ใช่การวินิจฉัยทางการแพทย์ และไม่ใช่การรับประกันผลสุขภาพ ผลสอบ หรือรายได้

Button labels change by intent:

- Health: `ส่งคำขอ Body & Routine Session`
- Licence & Care System: `ส่งคำขอ Exam & Care System Session`
- Open Conversation: `ส่งคำขอ Open Table Session`

#### Success

Use:

> **ได้รับคำขอนัดแล้ว 🍀**  
> เราจะติดต่อกลับเพื่อยืนยันเวลาอีกครั้ง

Show the reference number and all submitted details. Do not say “นัดกันแล้ว” until the requested time is confirmed.

#### Error

- Preserve every answer.
- Explain that the request was not sent.
- Offer a single `ลองส่งอีกครั้ง` action.
- Never leave the interface indefinitely in a sending state.

## 11. Conversation integrity

The interaction may include a short preparation indicator, but it must not imply that a human is typing live.

Allowed:

- `myClover กำลังเตรียมคำถามให้เหมาะกับคุณ…`
- Abstract dots or a Clover trace for 350–700 ms.

Not allowed:

- `Teem is typing…`
- `Ako กำลังตอบ…`
- Artificial delays longer than one second for ordinary transitions.
- Claims that AI has analysed or diagnosed the visitor.

## 12. Functional requirements

### P0 — Required for launch

- Preserve current `/api/meet` submission compatibility.
- Persist the selected intent from page selector into the booking flow.
- Support actual Gregorian date values and readable weekday labels.
- Support mode-specific durations.
- Validate required name and contact fields.
- Provide Back, Edit, Close, Escape, and retry behavior.
- Lock duplicate submissions while sending.
- Display explicit pending-confirmation status.
- Keep `/meet/admin/` data readable without breaking existing records.
- Work from `/meet/` and canonical trailing-slash URLs.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### P1 — After launch validation

- Calendar availability and confirmed slot reservation.
- LINE confirmation and reminder.
- Intent-specific preparation questions after submission.
- Intent-specific post-meeting destination.
- Optional video welcome from Teem and Ako.

Primary funnel:

`View → Intent → Booking open → Review → Completed request`

## 14. Success measures

Initial targets should be compared against the current `/meet` baseline.

- Increase intent selection rate.
- Increase booking-open to completed-request conversion.
- Reduce abandonment at contact entry.
- Reduce manual clarification required after submission.
- Track confirmation rate and actual meeting attendance separately from request completion.

Qualitative success:

- Visitors can explain what makes meeting Teem and Ako different.
- Visitors do not describe the page as a product or recruitment page.
- Visitors understand that no purchase, signup, health result, or income is guaranteed.

## 15. Accessibility, privacy, and performance

- Every action is usable with keyboard and screen reader.
- New messages are announced politely with an ARIA live region, not repeatedly.
- Focus moves to the new question and returns to the triggering CTA on close.
- Color is never the only indicator of selection or status.
- Tap targets are at least 44 × 44 px.
- Phone and LINE information are transmitted only to the existing booking endpoint.
- No third-party chat widget or tracking pixel is required for the conversational effect.
- Hero image remains the LCP priority; secondary images remain lazy-loaded.
- Motion must use transform and opacity where possible and avoid blocking interaction.

## 16. Acceptance criteria

The upgrade is ready for release when:

1. A first-time visitor understands the value of meeting both people from the first screen.
2. Each intent reveals specific takeaways and an honest boundary.
3. Booking completes through five conversational stages without page navigation.
4. The interface never pretends a human is typing live.
5. Dates, duration, request status, and confirmation language are accurate.
6. Existing API and admin records continue to work.
7. Mobile, desktop, keyboard, reduced-motion, success, retry, and close/reopen paths pass verification.
8. The page remains visually calm and materially faster than a heavy chat application.
