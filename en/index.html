CORE7 in thirty seconds

  • Each player picks seven cards — any mix of the four colors.
  • Both choose one card in secret, then reveal together.
  • 🔴 beats 🟢 · 🟢 beats 🔵 · 🔵 beats 🔴 · ⚙️ blocks every color.
  • The loser of a round discards the played card plus one more.
  • Win three rounds, or outlast the other hand.

There is no randomness anywhere in the game. Every result came from a decision one of you made.
