# Ako kitchen — everyday recipe provenance

Version: **1.0.0** · Compiled and source pages consulted: **2026-09-10**

This is implementation provenance for the eight additions in [`recipes.js`](../../ako/kitchen/recipes.js), not a new company source, licensed reproduction, nutrition assessment or record of kitchen testing. The existing seven recipes retain their original IDs, ingredient quantities and saved-state meaning. Shelf's catalog and README were checked; neither contains a recipe source to update.

The collection now contains 15 recipes. The eight new recipes are original Thai editorial adaptations of the cooking ideas below, with one-person base quantities. They use ordinary chicken, eggs, tofu, cabbage, cucumber, mushrooms, carrots and canned tuna. Ingredients are selected for accessibility; no current supermarket prices were checked, so no cost per serving is promised. The times are estimates for the base quantity, with cooked rice where stated. Cooking several portions can need more time or multiple pan batches.

## Preparation safety incorporated

- [FoodSafety.gov minimum internal temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures): poultry 74°C; egg dishes without meat 71°C. The chicken/egg bowl uses 74°C throughout. Color and elapsed cooking time are not treated as a substitute for the thermometer checks.
- [FoodSafety.gov four preparation steps](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety): separate raw poultry from ready-to-eat ingredients; wash hands and equipment; keep perishable ingredients refrigerated. The recipe text includes the directly relevant steps.
- Chilled tofu means a correctly refrigerated, ready-to-eat product as identified by its own label. No claim is made that every tofu sold in Thailand can be eaten directly from its package.
- Eggs, soy, wheat-containing soy sauce, sesame and fish are listed when used. Substitutions can change allergens; this is ingredient information, not an allergen-free certification or individualized dietary advice.
