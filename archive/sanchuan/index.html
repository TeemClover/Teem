Who We Are

Sanchuan Holdings is an international holding company and business incubator connecting China with Southeast Asia. Our flagship venture, Sanchuan Entertainment, leads in Thai-Chinese media co-productions and the rapidly growing vertical short drama (微短剧) market — handling script development, casting, high-end production, and platform distribution. We also pursue strategic investments in technology and infrastructure.
