Start with what is already special

Made for the table.
Not for notifications.

A phone can count life. HALO earns its place by feeling better: a compact object with a real rotary control, a circular touch display, quick setup, wireless linking and an interface that stays focused on the match.

Turn, don't hunt for buttons

The metal ring makes repeated life changes immediate and physical.

One unit or linked units

Use one HALO for 1, 2 or 4 players, or give every player their own linked device.

Quick setup by game

Profiles can load starting life and common utilities for popular games.

Undo and history

Correct mistakes while preserving a useful match trail.

Demonstrated product behavior

The simplest magic trick

I turn mine.
Yours knows.

Each player touches or rotates only the HALO in front of them while linked devices remain synchronized. That small interaction is what makes the product feel like gaming equipment rather than another app.

Wireless sync should be shown, not merely listed. Seeing 2 devices respond together sells the idea in seconds.

Competitive layer · proposed

The match should not disappear when it ends

The device can be borrowed.
The history should still be yours.

Stores should be able to lend HALO. Friends should be able to share HALO. But a competitive player's match history should follow the player profile — not the physical unit.

A deck is not one static object

Track the deck.
Track every version.

Competitive players change a few cards, test again, then compare. Treat each deck as a Series, then split it into Versions and Sessions so the app can answer the question that matters: did this change make the deck better?

Team intelligence · proposed

The biggest expansion opportunity

One player buys a tracker.
A team buys a training system.

When team members use their own profiles and sync during testing, the captain can see the group's work as one dataset: who tested what, against whom, with which version, and what changed.

Matchup coverage

See which important pairings are over-tested, under-tested or missing.

Version comparison

Measure whether a change improves actual team results.

Player testing activity

Understand training volume without manual logs after every game.

Captain dashboard

Turn scattered practice into one shared picture before the next major event.

Useful without AI. More useful with it.

The data is the product.
AI is the reader.

HALO does not need to build an expensive AI model. The app can calculate reliable facts itself, then export a clean analysis pack that players or teams bring to the AI service they already use.

Facts first

Win rate, duration, first/second split, matchup, deck version and life timeline.

Structured analysis

A fixed schema lets different AI tools read the same clean input.

Do not overclaim

A life curve proves when life changed. It cannot prove “you blocked too little” without richer context. Strategy remains interpretation.
