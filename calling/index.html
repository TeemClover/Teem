The tabletop experience

Not another
life-counter app.

A phone can count life. HALO changes how the table feels: physical input you can reach without unlocking a screen, shared information that stays synchronized, and a device whose only job is the match in front of you.

01

Turn the ring

Fast adjustment with a real metal control instead of repeated taps on a phone.

02

Touch when needed

A circular interface for setup, modes, confirmation and game utilities.

03

Link the table

Each player controls their own unit while everyone sees synchronized life state.

04

Carry it like gear

Compact hardware with a purpose-built travel case for game-store and tournament use.

Why hardware?

The phone works.
This feels better.

The strongest pitch is not that HALO can do things a phone cannot. It is that a purpose-built object can make the match cleaner, faster and more intentional.

Phone life tracker

  • Notifications and other apps live on the same screen
  • Usually one shared phone or multiple disconnected phones
  • Match state disappears when players close the app
  • Touch-only interaction
  • Built for everything, not specifically for the tabletop
VS

HALO

  • One purpose: stay with the match
  • Linked devices can share state wirelessly
  • Undo and history create a persistent match trail
  • Physical rotary input + touch
  • Designed as gaming gear you bring to the table

Competitive layer · proposed

The opportunity beyond life tracking

The match should not
disappear when it ends.

HALO already has the beginnings of something competitive players rarely get from a normal life app: a timestamped history of what changed. Add a small amount of match context and every practice game becomes analyzable training data.

01 · GAME

Choose format

FAB, MTG, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana or another profile.

02 · IDENTITY

Choose hero / leader

Starting life and identity can load from the selected character.

03 · MATCH

Play normally

Players only adjust state they already need to track.

04 · HISTORY

Keep the timeline

Life changes, time, first player, Undo and result become a match record.

05 · REVIEW

Find patterns

Use the mobile app to compare matchups and training sessions.

PATTERN, NOT A GUESS

When the device records clean events, the app can show what happened first. Strategic interpretation can remain a separate layer.

Data first

Useful without AI.
More useful with it.

The app does not need to build or pay for its own AI model. It can calculate the reliable numbers itself, then package selected match data in a structured analysis format that players can bring to the AI service they already use.

App calculates facts

Win rate, match duration, life timeline, first/second performance and matchup records.

AI interprets context

Players can ask for patterns, training priorities or session summaries — grounded in real logs.

Important boundary

Life data can show that damage was taken; it cannot by itself prove that a player “blocked too little” or “attacked too much.” Strategic conclusions should be labeled as interpretation unless richer match context exists.

A community-first path to market

Sell globally.
Let people try locally.

The product vision becomes stronger when stores are part of the distribution experience: players can discover HALO at the place they already gather, borrow one for a match and understand the value before buying.

Creator philosophy

Game-store lending pool

Place demo units at local stores so customers can borrow a HALO for the table. The device sells itself through the physical experience: the ring, the sync and the speed of setup.

Global + Thai community

One product, two access goals

Build for a worldwide tabletop audience while preserving a path for Thai players and stores to access the product at a locally reasonable level — with final pricing decided by the creator.

One player.

Built here. Aimed everywhere.

Thai-built hardware
deserves a global table.

The story is part of the product: software developed by the team, physical hardware brought to life through a local manufacturing path, and a tabletop experience good enough to be understood the moment someone turns the ring.

That is a more memorable campaign story than “another life tracker.” It is a story about a small team building the whole experience — physical and digital — and inviting the global tabletop community to use it.
