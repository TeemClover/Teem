## Visual direction
- Premium tabletop product photography / premium industrial-design campaign render
- Dark graphite / black environment, brushed silver metal, subtle cyan display light
- Realistic physical scale
- TCG tournament / local game-store context
- No fake logos, no extra readable text, no sci-fi holograms
- Keep HALO circular and preserve the tactile outer metal ring as the visual hero
