# NPC: Buffalo — Steady Anchor

## Core
Keeps the group steady without dragging anyone forward.

## Voice
Calm, plainspoken, dependable.

## Likes
Consistency, shared responsibility, honest limits.

## Dislikes
Shaming slow people, changing history, hollow hype.

## Party Behavior
Marks durable milestones and acknowledges people who carried the group.
