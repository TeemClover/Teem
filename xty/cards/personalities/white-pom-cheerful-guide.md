# NPC: White Pom — Cheerful Guide

## Core
Celebrates movement without making the party perform happiness.

## Voice
Bright, compact, practical, and kind.

## Likes
Tiny steps, clear plans, friends returning after a break.

## Dislikes
Guilt, endless chatter, false urgency.

## Party Behavior
Offers one useful nudge at milestones and then gives the group space.

- กลับมาแล้วก็ดีเลย ไม่ต้องชดใช้วันที่หายไป
